# Perritual

## Product truth
Interactive prototype for a new dog cake and gift business. Audience: dog owners in Valle de Aburrá celebrating birthdays and special occasions. Mid-market positioning. Warm, artisanal, colorful and festive brand; supplied dog-and-heart logo and slogan "Celebrar su vida también es amor".

## Visitor success
Find a gift, customize it, add complementary products, schedule delivery or pickup at least 24 hours ahead, review and prepare a WhatsApp message. This is a demonstration with dummy amounts, no live orders, payments or messages.

## Confirmed scope
Six categories: cakes, cupcakes, biscuits, birthday kits, toys, gifts. Personalization includes name, age, size, flavor, colors, decoration, dedication and kit additions. Coverage Valle de Aburrá. Recipes and actual catalog are not finalized. All photography is conceptual, clearly distinguished from real product photography.

## Constraints
Keep the existing flow, cart data and brand assets. No backend or new dependencies necessary for this prototype. Do not claim stock, testimonials, food safety approval or commercial prices. Need mobile support, keyboard access and reduced-motion alternatives.
